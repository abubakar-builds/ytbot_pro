FAQ

YouTube downloader questions

Is YTBOT free to use? ▼
Yes. YTBOT is free to use, with no daily download count limit for the normal browser download flow. You can paste links, check available formats, and download without creating an account.
Why do some 4K or 2K YouTube downloads have no sound? ▼
YouTube often stores high-resolution video and audio as separate streams. Basic downloaders might save only the video track. Our backend automatically uses FFmpeg to merge them into one playable MP4.
Can YTBOT convert YouTube to MP3? ▼
Yes. You can choose the "Audio Only (M4A/MP3)" option from the dropdown, and the system will extract the audio and convert it to an MP3 file using FFmpeg.
